Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful guidelines for patients visiting Shree J Hospital in Bibwewadi, Pune.

What are the OPD timings of Dr. Rajeev Joshi and Dr. Manasi Joshi?

Dr. Rajeev Joshi (Pediatrics) is available from 10:00 AM to 1:30 PM (Morning) and 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM (Evening). Dr. Manasi Joshi (Obstetrics & Gynecology) consults from 10:30 AM to 2:00 PM and 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM. On Sundays, limited morning OPD runs 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM. Emergency maternity cases are attended 24 hours.

Are all child vaccinations available at Shree J Hospital?

Yes, our vaccination clinic administers all Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) and WHO recommended vaccines (BCG, Polio, Hepatitis B, DTaP, MMR, Typhoid, Varicella, Pneumococcal, and Rotavirus). Vaccines are maintained under strict pharmaceutical cold-chain temperatures.

How can I make digital payments for hospital and OPD services?

You can click the "Pay Online" button on this website or scan the hospital UPI QR code at our reception counter using any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) or pay via Debit/Credit cards and NetBanking.

Is Shree J Hospital a registered center for MTP and Laparoscopic procedures?

Yes, Shree J Hospital is an authorized and registered healthcare facility for government-approved Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) with complete confidentiality. Our operation theatre is equipped for advanced minimally invasive laparoscopic gynecological and general surgeries.
